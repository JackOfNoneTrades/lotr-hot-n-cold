# Hot N Cold: LOTR Addon

![logo](src/main/resources/assets/hotncold/logo.png)

Hot N Cold is a Minecraft 1.7.10 addon for LOTR Legacy that gives modpack makers safer control over temperature, natural spawning, and NPC equipment.

## Features

- Configure frost and heat biomes and grant selected mobs immunity.
- Add LOTR biome temperatures to EnviroMine and EnviroMine Continuation.
- Generate Streams rivers, Greg Caves, and Wild Caves decorations in Middle-earth.
- Optionally mute lightning's explosion sound while keeping thunder and normal explosion sounds.
- Remove all natural animal spawns added by War of the Ring.
- Block selected mobs globally or in individual LOTR biomes.
- Add natural spawns with configurable category, frequency, and group size.
- Inspect, explain, generate, and reload spawn rules in-game.
- Randomize LOTR NPC melee weapons, ranged weapons, shields, and armor.
- Optionally apply NPC gear rules to LOTR spawn eggs, structures, and invasions.
- Target exact NPCs, custom groups, factions, or every LOTR NPC.
- Preserve existing, hired, named, quest-linked, and location-specific NPCs by default.
- Use LOTR biomes in Custom NPCs.
- Remove the War of the Ring dedicated-server restriction.
- Diagnose blocked spawns and applied NPC equipment with optional logging.

For installation, configuration examples, commands, migration help, and troubleshooting, read the [Hot N Cold wiki](https://github.com/JackOfNoneTrades/lotr-hot-n-cold/wiki).

## Dependencies

- [LOTR Legacy](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-lord-of-the-rings-mod-legacy) is required.
- [UniMixins](https://modrinth.com/mod/unimixins) is required.
- [EnviroMine](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/enviromine) is optional.
- War of the Ring, Mo' Creatures, IvToolkit, Custom NPCs, and Bad Mobs integrations are optional.
- Streams/Farseek, Greg Caves/Mycelium, and Wild Caves 3 are optional world-generation integrations.
